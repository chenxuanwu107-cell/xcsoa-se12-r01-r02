# Requirements handoff for complex decisions

Read only when a short requirements note cannot carry the actual decision.
This is a portable documentation convention, not a production schema.

Identify the exact target, revision, representation, and relevant span. Use a
content hash when exact byte identity matters; it does not prove authority,
truth, or current applicability. Preserve source → claim → method support and
reading depth: original bytes, selected section, abstract, secondary report, or
unknown. A citation without accessible content cannot support a claim that the
full source was reviewed.

Keep original words and interpretation separate. Identify effective obligations,
audience, purpose, exceptions, relevant time, known facts, conflicts, and
unknowns. A conflict requires incompatible requirements over the same subject,
scope, and time. An external incident is conditional evidence; it does not prove
that the current project has the same defect. State its necessary premise and
the local evidence, or mark applicability unknown.

Record an actual execution reference or NOT_RUN, the actual consumer/use, and
the smallest next evidence plus owner. Do not duplicate protected logs to make
the envelope look complete. A sanitized projection must say what was omitted
and what that prevents the reviewer from concluding.

Acceptance for review can be valid even when execution is NOT_RUN. It does not
establish business acceptance, verification, or release authority. If R02 is
available, it can consume this note as its requirements input; otherwise give it
directly to the implementer, designer, evaluator, or authorized decision maker.
Neither route requires a fixed role hierarchy.
