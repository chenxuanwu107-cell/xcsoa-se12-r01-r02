All source IDs below refer to that ledger, not public download links. Original
files stay private; hashes enable comparison by an authorized custodian and are
not independent signatures or proof of copyright ownership.

Internal role and platform names are translated to existing functional owners
only where needed. Permission enforcement, execution state, evaluation, and
recovery stay separate responsibilities. This is not a description of private
production architecture. Ordinary users need neither internal identifiers nor
access to original archives.

The release does not include PM/Tutor textbooks, external skill corpora,
third-party source code, papers, prototype runtimes, internal schema bodies,
grants, original logs, private chats, or an internal Git database. Optional
research catalogs are omitted rather than converted into unsupported extra
capabilities. No benchmark or evidence count is promoted into method efficacy.

The adaptation is readable and reviewable, but equivalence is a maintainer's
source comparison, not independent professional qualification. Historical
instructions such as stopping research rounds are treated as source scope;
they do not authorize or cancel the user's present release-preparation task.
